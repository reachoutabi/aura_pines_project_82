Born in the Ancient Canopies

The Aura Pines Story

Founded on the belief that human spirits flourish when reconnected to the quiet majesty of pristine pine forests and starlit night skies.

Our Heritage

Rooted in Conservation, Crafted for Wonder

In 2012, a group of alpine mountaineers, naturalists, and architects set out to create a sanctuary where travellers could experience the unvarnished beauty of the high country without causing an ounce of degradation to its fragile ecosystem.

Today, Aura Pines encompasses 450 private acres bordering the national wilderness reserve. We designed our shelters using low-impact screw-pile foundations that touch the earth with gentle precision, ensuring not a single mature cedar or Douglas fir was felled during construction.

100% Off-Grid Solar micro-grid with zero noisy diesel generators
Zero Waste Compost systems & complete single-use plastic ban
Milestones

The Wilderness Journey

2012
Whispering Ridge Acquired

Land Stewardship Trust Established

450 acres of degraded former timberland placed under perpetual environmental easement to allow forest regeneration.

2016
First 6 Safari Tents

Eco-Glamping Welcomes First Guests

Debuted custom weather-sealed canvas tents powered by small off-grid solar arrays and gravity-fed spring water.

2020
Architectural Innovation

Geodesic Stargazing Domes Completed

Engineered high-altitude geometric dome shelters featuring insulated thermal skins and crystal panoramic skylights.

2024
Global Recognition

Awarded National Wilderness Excellence

Certified by Leave No Trace International and named Best Sustainable Glamping Destination by Eco-Traveler.
