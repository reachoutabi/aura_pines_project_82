# AURA PINES - Camping Ground & Adventure Camp HTML5 Template

A premium, outdoorsy, and immersive Camping Ground & Adventure Camp HTML Template designed for ThemeForest, TemplateMonster, and high-end outdoor hospitality clients.

## Key Features
- **Design Palette**: "Midnight Pine & Ember" (`#0B1612`, `#D2824A`, `#4F8F8A`, `#EFE7D6`)
- **Theme Modes**: Full Dark Mode (default) & Warm Parchment Light Mode
- **Bi-directional Support**: Complete RTL layout support with CSS logical properties
- **5-Step Booking Engine**: Dates, accommodation selector, add-on provisions, guest details, coupon codes, and printable confirmation
- **Guest Portal Dashboard**: Upcoming trip countdown, live weather widget, spend & activity charts (Chart.js), bookings management with cancellation & invoice downloads, packing checklist, and day-by-day itinerary planner
- **100% Frontend**: Pure HTML5, Bootstrap 5.3.3, Vanilla ES6 JavaScript, and LocalStorage persistence. Zero backend required.

## Included Pages
1. `index.html` - Homepage 1: Glamping Sanctuary (interactive booking bar, timeline, stats counter, weather widget & reviews)
2. `index-2.html` - Homepage 2: Alpine Expeditions & Adventure Basecamp (trail finder, summit routes & field academy)
3. `packages.html` - Curated retreat packages with duration/group filters, sorting, and side-by-side comparison drawer
3. `activities.html` - 12 outdoor activities catalog with difficulty filter, category tabs, and safety guides
4. `booking.html` - Multi-step reservation wizard with coupon `EMBER10` and invoice download
5. `login.html` - Split-screen guest authentication portal (Demo user: `guest@campwild.com` / `Guest@123`)
6. `dashboard.html` - Comprehensive guest dashboard with tabs, charts, table filtering, and packing list
7. `about.html` - Wilderness sanctuary story, conservation pledge, guides team, and milestones timeline
8. `gallery.html` - Filterable masonry photo gallery with full interactive lightbox
9. `contact.html` - Trailhead access directions, GPS coordinates, ranger dispatch hotline & contact form
10. `404.html` - Themed "Lost in the woods?" error page with animated compass
11. `coming-soon.html` - Winter season launch countdown timer with email signup
12. `documentation/index.html` - Complete developer and buyer manual
