Our Sacred Pledge

Preserving the Wilderness for Generations

Aura Pines operates on 100% solar micro-grids, zero single-use plastics, and strict Leave No Trace environmental standards. 5% of every reservation is directed directly to alpine watershed restoration.

100% Solar Powered Grid
Quiet battery storage banks power all lighting, heated blankets, and bathhouse water heaters with zero generator noise.
Natural Watershed Protection
All greywater is filtered through sub-surface biological reed beds before returning to the mountain stream.
Wildlife Corridor Respect
All trails and tent clusters are positioned strictly outside migratory elk and deer feeding corridors.
Leave No Trace Certified Official Partner of the National Wilderness Trust
Answers Before You Trek

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about weather, packing, parking, campfires, and reservations.

What is provided inside each tent or dome?
All Luxury Domes and Safari Tents come fully furnished with plush memory-foam mattresses, premium organic cotton linens, goose-down duvets, warm wool blankets, solar lamps, bedside tables, and a private outdoor fire pit with chairs. Domes also include indoor pellet heaters and private ensuite composting washrooms.
How do I reach the campsite? Do I need a 4x4 vehicle?
Our base parking lot is accessible by standard 2WD vehicles on well-maintained paved national forest roads. From the parking lot, our solar electric safari shuttles transfer you and your luggage directly to your tent deck. For backpacker pitches, there is a scenic 1.2-mile footpath.
Are campfires allowed, and where do I get firewood?
Yes! Each stay includes a designated cast-iron fire ring with a spark arrestor. You can pre-order sustainably sourced, pest-free kiln-dried firewood bundles through our booking flow or guest portal. For environmental preservation, foraging wood from the surrounding living forest is prohibited.
What is the cancellation policy?
Reservations cancelled at least 7 days prior to check-in receive a 100% full refund with zero cancellation fee. Cancellations made between 3 to 7 days before arrival receive a 75% refund or a 100% wilderness credit valid for 18 months. You can cancel directly from your Guest Dashboard in one click.
Are pets allowed at Aura Pines?
Well-behaved dogs are warmly welcome in our Riverside Pitches, Family Cabins, and select Safari Tents! We provide a dog welcome kit with a collapsible water bowl, organic treats, and a trail map of dog-friendly paths. Please keep pets on a leash in communal camp areas.
